# Sales Frameworks (research notes)

Attribution: lancewc/sales-claude-skills (https://github.com/lancewc/sales-claude-skills), README and skill library. All framework names below are the source's own; the Forge draft restates the method in its own words and adds evidence-per-stage verification the source does not require.

## The six skills in the source library

- gap-selling-discovery: dissect Current State, audit call transcripts, ladder the 3 Whys, calculate the Cost of Inaction (COI). Framework: Keenan's GAP Selling.
- meddpicc-deal-control: score active pipeline 0-16 points, test Champions vs. Coaches with 3 acid tests, identify Economic Buyers, map legal Paper Processes. Framework: John McMahon's MEDDPICC.
- great-demo-architecture: kill the linear harbor tour; script demos that show the executive payoff screen in Minute 3 with at most 3 clicks to value. Framework: Peter Cohan's Great Demo!
- jolt-indecision-resolver: overcome buyer Fear Of Messing Up (FOMU); diagnose Information Overload vs. Career Risk; script procurement concession trades. Framework: Dixon and McKenna's The JOLT Effect.
- cre-saas-cfo-memo: translate desk occupancy and space right-sizing into capitalized balance-sheet OpEx reductions and 1-page CFO board memos. Framework: FASB ASC 842 and CRE accounting.
- outbound-pipeline-provocation: provocative 4-sentence executive emails, cold call pattern interrupts, multi-threading scripts. Framework: the source's Provocation Outbound Engine.

## The source's thesis, in its own terms

Average rep (charisma) pitches features, gives 60-minute harbor tours, sells to friendly coaches, discounts under pressure, and loses 60 percent to "No Decision". Elite AE (engineering) quantifies Cost of Inaction, shows the payoff screen in minute 3, tests true champions, trades concessions, and de-risks buyer FOMU. The difference is financial literacy, diagnostic rigor, and deal control.

## The 18-module curriculum behind it

Modules cover OpEx vs. CapEx and EBITDA, the SaaS financial engine (CAC, LTV, NRR), cost of inaction, the 5 dimensions of current state, the Rule of 3 Whys, reframing feature requests, the champion protocol, economic buyer alignment, decision criteria and paper process, the inverted pyramid demo, fewest clicks to value, delta storytelling, mapping 4 buyer personas, triangulating enterprise value, the mutual action plan, FOMU vs. FOMO, the JOLT protocol, and concession trading.

## What the Forge draft takes from this

The cycle shape (discover, qualify, demo, de-risk indecision, close with a mutual plan) and the named methods (GAP Selling, MEDDPICC 16-point audit, Great Demo, JOLT, concession trading). The draft does not copy any skill text or textbook. It adds the Forge rules: evidence per pipeline stage, unknown written as unknown, no outbound message without operator approval of exact text and recipient, and a shipped pipeline report as the output.
