# Platform Limits

Per-platform limits and norms for the five Typefully platforms in `social-production`. Limits change; treat this file as the working copy and verify against the platform when a post is rejected or truncated. Character counts are per post unless noted.

## X

- 280 characters per post. Threads chain posts; each post in the thread must stand on its own.
- Links count toward the limit. Put the link in the post only when it must travel with the text; otherwise use a follow-up post.
- 3 to 10 posts a day is the working frequency. More than that reads as noise.
- Images and video attach to a post; alt text is worth writing.
- X ranking rewards replies and dwell time. A thread that earns replies beats a thread that earns likes.

## LinkedIn

- 3,000 characters per post. Longer posts get cut behind "see more"; the hook must land before the cut.
- 3 to 5 posts a week. Daily posting is fine only when the quality holds.
- Carousels go out as document posts (PDF). Keep slides legible on a phone screen.
- External links in the post body reduce reach. Put the link in the first comment.
- No em dashes in posts. Use a period or a line break.

## Threads

- 500 characters per post.
- 1 to 2 posts a day. Threads is conversational; replies are the format, not an afterthought.
- Links in posts are followed normally. Keep them to one per post.
- Hashtags: zero to two, integrated naturally. Hashtag stacks read as spam here.

## Bluesky

- 300 characters per post. Threads chain posts; keep each post complete.
- 1 to 3 posts a day.
- Links get rich cards automatically. One link per post.
- Starter packs and feeds matter for discovery. Post where the audience's feeds are.

## Mastodon

- 500 characters per post by default. The exact limit depends on the instance; check the instance before writing long posts.
- 1 to 2 posts a day.
- Content warnings exist for a reason: use them for sensitive topics, spoilers, and long threads.
- Hashtags are the discovery mechanism on Mastodon. Two to four relevant tags per post is normal here, unlike the other platforms.
- Federation means the audience is spread across instances. Write for the topic community, not for one server.

## Cross-Posting Rules

- One idea, five platform versions. Adapt format, length, and tone per platform. Never paste the same text everywhere and call it distribution.
- A 280 character X post does not become a LinkedIn post by adding a line break. Rewrite it.
- A LinkedIn carousel does not become an X post. The thread is the X version; the carousel is the LinkedIn version.
- Track the trace: every post in the schedule names its platform, format, scheduled time, and status.
