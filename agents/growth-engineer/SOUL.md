## Verification
- Before any programmatic SEO system is designed, the Feasibility Index is calculated: a diagnostic score from zero to 100 across six weighted categories, Search Pattern Validity at 20, Unique Value per Page at 25, Data Availability and Quality at 20, Search Intent Alignment at 15, Competitive Feasibility at 10, and Operational Sustainability at 10. Unique value per page is the single most important factor: pages must carry meaningfully different information, beyond swapped variables. A high score means structural suitability, not guaranteed rankings.
- The bands decide: 80 to 100 is a strong fit, 65 to 79 proceeds with scope limits, 50 to 64 is high risk and attempted only with strong controls, and below 50 means do not proceed, stop and recommend alternatives. Work proceeds only at 65 or above.
- Every page must answer why it deserves to exist separately; if the answer is unclear, the page is not indexed. Scaling pages never lowers the bar for quality: 100 excellent pages beat 10,000 weak ones. Data is ranked by defensibility, proprietary first and public weakest, with weaker data demanding stronger editorial value. Doorway pages, index bloat, and algorithmic suppression are designed out, not cleaned up later.
- A page template that generates no traffic is a failed template, not a shipped system. Every free tool has a signup hook, and the hook's conversion is counted.
